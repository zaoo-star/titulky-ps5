# Xbox

<a id="ucet"></a>
**Account:** with an Xbox, ReCaption signs in with **the Microsoft account you play with on the Xbox** (the one whose Xbox
profile is on the console). A second account like on PlayStation isn’t needed here – ReCaption doesn’t control anything and
the controller stays with the player. E-mail and password are typed **only on Microsoft’s page** (microsoft.com/link) on a
phone or computer, never on the TV or into ReCaption.

1. On the Xbox: **Settings → Devices & connections → Remote features** → turn on **Enable remote features**.
2. **Settings → General → Power mode**: choose **Sleep** (otherwise the Xbox can’t be turned on remotely).
3. In ReCaption: the **Console** page (titled Xbox for an Xbox) → **Sign in with a Microsoft account**. A code appears on the screen.
4. On a phone or computer open **[microsoft.com/link](https://www.microsoft.com/link)** (or scan the QR code
   on the screen; on a phone with ReCaption the **Open the page** button), enter the code and sign in with the account from “Account” above.
5. ReCaption shows the Xboxes on the account – choose the one you play on (a single one is picked automatically).

It works the same on Android TV, on a phone used as the reader for an LG / Samsung TV, and from an iPhone (the iPhone only opens
microsoft.com/link). ReCaption for PC doesn’t connect to the Xbox itself – it can only read the window in which the game runs on the PC.

If the link doesn’t work, search for **“Xbox remote play enable remote features”**.
