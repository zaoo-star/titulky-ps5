# DeepL – better translation (free with your own key)

The key is **never typed on the TV with the remote** – it is copied and pasted only on a phone (Android or iPhone).
In ReCaption: the setup guide → step **Better translation (DeepL)**, or any time **Subtitles → Translation quality**.
The TV shows **one QR code** – once scanned with a phone (on the **same Wi‑Fi**), the page
“ReCaption · DeepL” opens and walks you through all three steps below.

> **Already have a DeepL account (even just for translating)?** First **sign in** on deepl.com and then on the pricing page,
> on the **API** tab, tap **Sign up for free** on the **Developer** plan – the plan is added to that account.
> The “Get a free API key” / “Free API” links in DeepL’s menu send signed-in users to the Translator.

1. **Free DeepL API account** (first time only):
   👉 **[DeepL pricing – API tab](https://www.deepl.com/pro#api)** → on the **Developer** plan the
   **Sign up for free** button; the cookie bar can be closed with **Reject**
   - without an account: fill in **E-mail** and **Password** (at least 8 characters), under **Your plan** keep **Developer** (Growth is paid),
     leave the newsletter box empty, choose **Continue** and **confirm your e-mail** with the link DeepL sends,
   - with a DeepL account (even Translator only): **sign in** first, then the same button – the plan is added to the account,
   - the Developer plan is free: **1,000,000 characters in total** (it doesn’t renew each month), no payment card needed,
   - if sign-up hangs on **“Security check”**, turn off VPN or ad blocking (on iPhone also iCloud Private
     Relay), or try another browser or mobile data.
2. **Copy the key**:
   👉 **[Keys page](https://www.deepl.com/your-account/keys)** – the **API Keys & Limits** tab.
   If you see **Log in to your account** instead of keys, sign in with the account from step 1 (e-mail and password, or Google / Apple,
   whichever the account was created with) – then the tab opens.
   The full key isn’t shown there: copy it with the **copy icon** next to the key. If there is no key, choose **Create key**.
3. **Paste the key** (back on the “ReCaption · DeepL” page from the TV): **hold your finger in the field →
   Paste** → **Send to TV**.
4. Done – the TV checks the key, saves it and turns DeepL on (“✔ DeepL is set up”).

If the link doesn’t work: on deepl.com open **Pricing** and switch to the **API** tab.

**How to tell it works:** in ReCaption under Subtitles → Translation quality you see “The key works. … of 1,000,000 characters left.”
and the **Check key again** button. When DeepL isn’t available (no internet, limit used up, key rejected), translation continues
offline and the reason is shown in the same place. After the 1,000,000 characters are used up, only offline translation is left (or a paid DeepL plan).
A paid key (API Pro / Growth) works too.
With DeepL turned on, the recognized subtitle text is sent to DeepL.
