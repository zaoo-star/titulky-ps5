# Phone – Android and iPhone

What each phone can do:

| | Android | iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| Set up an Android TV (sign-in, number from the PS5, DeepL) | ✔ in the ReCaption app or in the browser | ✔ in Safari |
| Install ReCaption on an Android TV | ✔ | – (Downloader on the TV) |
| Read and translate for an LG / Samsung TV | ✔ | – (computer: [Windows](pc.md) / [Mac](mac.md)) |
| Install ReCaption on an LG / Samsung TV | ✔ | – (computer) |

Path by TV: [Getting started with ReCaption](zacit.md).

<a id="android"></a>
## Android – installing ReCaption

1. On the phone open this link and download ReCaption:
   https://recaption.pages.dev/d/latest/ReCaption.apk
2. Open the downloaded file (the download notification, or the **Files** app → Downloads). If the phone asks,
   allow the browser to **Install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → the browser, e.g. Chrome →
   **Install unknown apps** → Allow) and go Back.
3. **Install** → **Open**. The phone shows **Sign-in for the TV** (the helper), not the full app.

In **Sign-in for the TV** you then have:
- **Android TV:** on the TV choose **Set up with a phone or computer**, scan the QR code with the camera and on the page choose
  **In the ReCaption app**. Installing on the TV: the **Install ReCaption on Android TV** button ([guide](android-tv.md#telefon)).
- **LG / Samsung TV:** the **Read for the TV** button – the phone becomes
  the reader and the setup guide opens (9 steps; installing on the TV is in the Television step). Back to the helper: ⚙ Settings →
  General → **Sign-in helper**.

<a id="pozadi"></a>
## Android as the reader while playing (LG / Samsung)

- Keep the phone **on the charger** and on the same Wi‑Fi as the PS5 and the TV.
- On the first **Start**, ReCaption asks about running in the background → **Allow**. Later: in ReCaption ⚙ Settings →
  General → **Allow background use**, or on the phone Settings → Apps → ReCaption → Battery → **Unrestricted**.
  Otherwise the phone may put it to sleep after a while.
- **Samsung phones** also put background apps to sleep their own way – if the TV shows the orange "reading is slow"
  line or the translation stops after a while: Settings → **Battery and device care** → **Battery** →
  **Background usage limits** → **Never sleeping apps** → **+** → add ReCaption. Quicker: in ReCaption ⚙ Settings →
  General → **Never put to sleep (Samsung)** opens that screen directly (the row shows only on Samsung phones).

<a id="iphone"></a>
## iPhone

There is no ReCaption for iPhone and none is needed. The iPhone works as a helper:
- **Android TV setup:** on the TV choose **Set up with a phone or computer** and scan the QR code with the
  **Camera** app (same Wi‑Fi). **ReCaption · TV settings** opens in Safari: sign-in with the 🔵 ReCaption account
  (on the almost empty “redirect” page tap the address at the top → **Copy** → go back to the ReCaption tab,
  or without copying use the [ReCaption shortcut](zkratka.md)), the number from the PS5 (hold your finger in the field → **Scan Text**)
  and the DeepL key.
- **Signing in for ReCaption on a computer or on the TV:** scan the QR code, sign in with Sony and copy the “redirect”
  address the same way.
- **Problem report:** in ReCaption ⚙ Settings → General → **Create a problem report** →
  **To phone** → scan the QR code → download the report → send the file (Files → Downloads → Share).
- **Links from the app** (LG, PS5…) open in Safari.

An iPhone can’t read subtitles and send them to an LG / Samsung TV – that is done by an Android phone or box, or
a computer ([Windows](pc.md) / [Mac](mac.md)).
